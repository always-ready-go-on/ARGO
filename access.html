Google sign-in access

ARGO's Google sign-in is invite-only for now. If Google showed you "Access blocked" or Error 403: access_denied when you signed in, your Google account hasn't been invited yet.

Ask for access

Email us the Google account address you want to connect:

Email sr.argo.v2@gmail.com

Once you're added, usually within a few days, sign in to Google in ARGO again. We use the address only to add it to the invite list, and remove it from the list when you ask.

While ARGO is invite-only, Google ends an invited person's sign-in after 7 days. ARGO then asks you to connect Google again.

Or use your own Google Cloud client (computers only)

On macOS, Windows or Linux you can skip the invite list by signing in through a Google Cloud project of your own. It's free and takes about ten minutes:

  1. In Google Cloud Console, create a project.
  2. Under APIs & Services → Library, enable the Google Calendar, Google Tasks, Gmail and Google Drive APIs (only the ones you want).
  3. Under Google Auth Platform, set up the consent screen with audience External, and add your own Google address under Audience → Test users.
  4. Under Clients, create an OAuth client of type Desktop app.
  5. In ARGO's Google step, choose Advanced: use your own Google Cloud client, and paste the client ID and secret.

Android signs in through Google Play services with ARGO's own app identity, so on a phone the invite list is the only way in for now.
