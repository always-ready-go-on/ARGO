A personal AI agent that runs on your own devices

ARGO is an app for your computer (macOS, Windows, Linux) and your Android phone. It reads, searches and gets things done on your behalf, and remembers what matters to you, on your devices rather than on a server of ours.

What ARGO does

Works for you

Ask in plain words. ARGO searches, reads, browses and carries out tasks on the device it runs on, asking before anything it cannot undo.

Remembers on your device

Conversations and what ARGO learns about you are stored locally. You can review and delete them at any time.

Your choice of AI model

You connect the AI model provider you want to use, with your own account and key.

Connecting Google

If you choose to, you can connect your Google account so ARGO can help with:

  • Google Calendar: see your calendars and events, and create or change events when you ask.
  • Google Tasks: see and update your task lists.
  • Gmail: search and read your email (read only; ARGO does not send, delete or change mail).
  • Google Drive: find and read your files (read only).

You pick which of these to allow on Google's own consent screen, and you can disconnect at any time. ARGO uses this data only to do what you ask; it is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models. The details are in our Privacy Policy.

ARGO's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
